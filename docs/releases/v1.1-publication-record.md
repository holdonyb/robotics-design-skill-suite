## Evidence and authority boundary

v1.1.0 validates only retained, externally supplied authority evidence. Its
`external_human_attestation` record must match a recorded commissioning phase's
design digest, date, location, reachable emergency stop, people, limits, and
watchdog timeout. This check remains evidence-only: no local record, CI result,
simulation result, release, or hash authorizes procurement, fabrication,
energization, device access, or robot motion. The release report continues to
derive `hardware_claims: false`.
