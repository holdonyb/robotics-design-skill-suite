## Claim boundary

Passing the live gate means the pinned Linux ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Harmonic,
ros2_control, MoveIt, and Nav2 consumers loaded together for the reference
workspace. It does not show that a motion plan was executed safely, a scenario
was completed, a controller was tuned, a calibration reflects a physical robot,
or any component has supplier/bench/hardware evidence.

No v0.5 action authorizes purchasing, fabrication, energizing, or moving a
robot. v0.6 onward must add supplier-authenticated parts, bench measurements,
controlled integration, safety procedures, and explicit human motion authority
before any real-hardware claim can be considered.
