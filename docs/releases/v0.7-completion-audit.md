## Claim boundary

No v0.7 action authorizes purchasing, fabrication, energizing, movement, or
device access. Real bench evidence still requires externally authorized
equipment, a controlled site, qualified operators, a reachable E-stop, bounded
energy, an approved test card, instrument calibration and original retained
data. `fixture_only` proves parser behavior only.
