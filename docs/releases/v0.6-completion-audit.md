## Claim boundary

No v0.6 action authorizes purchasing, fabrication, vendor commitment,
energizing, or moving a robot. A future physical phase requires appropriate
budget and account authority, selected and reviewed parts, controlled site,
reachable E-stop, named operators, bounded energy, explicit motion authority,
and retained raw measurements. This release supplies only the fail-closed
record and review machinery needed to make those dependencies visible.
